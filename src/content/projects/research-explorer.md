## The idea

Research Explorer is the working label for a literature research web application using OpenAlex-related workflows. It brings literature discovery and filtering together with DOI, PDF, and CSV features.

## Areas of focus

- Exploring literature through OpenAlex-related research workflows.
- Filtering results to narrow a research question.

- Using CSV features within the literature research workflow.
